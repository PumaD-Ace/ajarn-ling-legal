Privacy Policy

This policy covers Ajarn Ling Publishing, a small internal software tool used to publish pre-produced episodes of the "Ajarn Ling" Thai traffic-education video series to the @ajarnling accounts on YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram.

Who this tool acts on behalf of

This tool is operated by a single individual, the creator of the Ajarn Ling series, for their own accounts only. It is not offered to, or used by, any other person or business. It does not act on behalf of the public or any third-party user.

What data it accesses

Where a platform's API requires OAuth authorization (for example TikTok's Content Posting API, scope video.publish), this tool only requests the minimum permission needed to upload a video file and its title/description to the single @ajarnling account that authorized it. It does not read, collect, or store any other account data (followers, messages, analytics, or personal information of any viewer or third party).

What data it stores

The tool stores, locally on the operator's own machine, the OAuth access/refresh tokens needed to stay authenticated between publishing runs, and a local log of which episode was published where and when. Nothing is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by a third party other than the platforms being published to (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram) and their respective APIs.

What data it does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for any purpose beyond publishing the operator's own video content.
  • No accounts other than the operator's own are ever accessed.

This website

This is a static, no-JavaScript informational page. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard, anonymized web-server access logs as any static site host does; this project does not access or use those logs.
